Our Story

From a Small Workshop to Australia's Trusted Name

Ironclad Door Hardware was founded in 2005 by a team of locksmiths and builders who saw a gap in the market: there was plenty of cheap hardware around, but finding products that combined lasting durability with thoughtful design meant dealing with dozens of fragmented suppliers.

We set out to become a single source for premium door hardware — curating the best from global manufacturers and backing every product with genuine technical expertise. Today we supply architects, builders, property developers, and homeowners from our Brisbane warehouse and showroom.

Every item in our range is hand-selected and tested against Australian standards before it reaches our shelves. We don't just sell products — we help you specify the right solution for the job, whether that's a heritage lever set for a Queenslander or a high-security access system for a commercial tower.
